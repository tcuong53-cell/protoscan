7 automated checks
Catch issues that manual QA misses
CriticalDead-end screens
Users tap a button and nothing happens. No way out, no back, no close. The prototype breaks.
CriticalOverlay traps
A modal opens but has no close button. Users are stuck staring at a dialog they can't dismiss.
HighOrphan screens
Screens that exist but can't be reached. Disconnected from the flow, invisible to testers.
HighSmall touch targets
Interactive elements under 44x44px. Users fumble trying to tap tiny buttons on mobile.
MediumMissing back navigation
Users navigate into a screen but can't go back. Forward-only flows frustrate testers.
MediumOverlapping hotspots
Two tap areas overlap. Users accidentally trigger the wrong action and lose trust in the prototype.
MediumMissing scroll
Content extends beyond the screen but scroll isn't enabled. Important information is hidden.

FAQ
Is my Figma data safe?
ProtoScan reads your prototype structure (screens, connections, bounding boxes) through the official Figma REST API. It never stores your designs, images, or file content. All processing happens locally in your terminal or in-memory on our servers.
Do I need a paid Figma plan?
No. ProtoScan works with any Figma plan, including the free tier. You just need a Personal Access Token (free to generate at figma.com/settings).
What does the runtime simulator do?
The Pro simulator opens your prototype in a headless browser and clicks through every screen like a real user. It detects issues that static analysis can't catch: buttons that don't navigate, broken transitions, and timing-dependent flows. You get a video walkthrough of the entire prototype.
Can I cancel Pro anytime?
Yes, cancel anytime from your Polar.sh dashboard. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your Pro features stay active until the end of the billing period.
Does it work with private Figma files?
Yes. Pass your Figma Personal Access Token via the FIGMA_TOKEN environment variable. The token gives ProtoScan read-only access to files you can already see in Figma.

7 automated checks
Catch issues that manual QA misses
CriticalDead-end screens
Screens with no outgoing links, back, or close actions. Users get stuck with no way out.
CriticalOverlay traps
Overlays that open but have no close or back action. Users are trapped in a modal.
HighOrphan screens
Screens that can't be reached from any flow starting point. Disconnected from the prototype.
HighSmall touch targets
Interactive elements smaller than 44x44px. Hard to tap on mobile devices.
MediumMissing back navigation
Screens with incoming links but no back action. Users can navigate in but not out.
MediumOverlapping hotspots
Interactive elements with overlapping tap areas. Users may accidentally trigger the wrong action.
MediumMissing scroll
Frames with content extending beyond bounds but scroll not enabled. Content is hidden.

Ask Claude to scan your prototype. Works in Claude Desktop and claude.ai.
